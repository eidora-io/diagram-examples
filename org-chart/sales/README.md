# Sales Team Org Chart

![Sales team org chart example: a VP of Sales over a sales manager with seven account executives in one box, and a sales operations analyst](preview.webp)

A sales team chart with a VP, a sales manager leading seven account executives, and an analyst who supports the team. When many people share a role and have no reports of their own, they are grouped in one box with their names. This keeps a large team compact.

## Input text

```text
Sales team: Laura Kim is VP of Sales. Sales manager Omar Haddad reports to her and leads seven account executives: Ann, Ben, Cal, Dee, Eve, Fay and Gus. A sales operations analyst also reports to Laura.
```
