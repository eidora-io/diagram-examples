# Flat Org Chart

![Flat org chart example: a founder with a designer, two engineers, a marketer and a customer support lead reporting directly to her](preview.webp)

A flat org chart: a founder working directly with a designer, two engineers, a marketer and a support lead, in a single row. Suits small teams that move fast. A growing row is a sign that it may be time to add a team lead.
