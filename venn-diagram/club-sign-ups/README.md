# Two Lists: Who Is in Both Clubs?

![Venn diagram example made from two lists: chess club and drama club members, with Chloe and Emma in both clubs](preview.webp)

Two lists of club members sorted into a Venn diagram, with the people who joined both clubs in the overlap. Nothing is added and no name is dropped. Works for any two lists, such as customers and products.

## Input text

```text
Who signed up for which club?
Chess club: Ava, Ben, Chloe, Daniel, Emma, Finn
Drama club: Chloe, Emma, Grace, Henry, Isla
```

The same text is in [source.txt](source.txt). Made with the [Venn Diagram Maker](https://www.eidora.io/tools/venn-diagram-maker).
