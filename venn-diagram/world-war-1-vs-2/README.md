# World War I vs World War II Venn Diagram

![Venn diagram example: World War I vs World War II, with trench warfare and 1914 to 1918 for WWI, atomic bombs and 1939 to 1945 for WWII, and tanks, airplanes and millions of casualties in the overlap](preview.webp)

A history comparison of the two world wars, with each war's dates and defining events on its own side and shared features in the overlap. The overlap becomes the similarities paragraph of an essay. A planning aid for history essays.

## Input text

```text
Compare World War I and World War II
```

The same text is in [source.txt](source.txt). Made with the [Venn Diagram Maker](https://www.eidora.io/tools/venn-diagram-maker).
