# Business Concept Map: a Meal-Kit Startup

![Business concept map example for a meal-kit startup: customers, recipes, subscriptions, ingredients from local farms, the kitchen and the delivery partner](preview.webp)

A business concept map of a meal-kit startup: customers, the app, recipes, local farms, the kitchen and the delivery partner. Following the arrows shows the loop from customer reviews to new recipes. Handy for explaining a business to a new hire or an investor.

## Input text

```text
Our meal-kit startup: customers choose recipes on the app and subscribe weekly. We buy ingredients from local farms, pack them in our kitchen, and a delivery partner brings the boxes. Subscriptions pay for everything; customer reviews help us pick new recipes.
```
